      Before we build

      • Landlord OK. A fixed wall in the leased unit needs the landlord's approval and may need a City of Richmond permit.
      • Sprinkler, air, light. Check the closed-off corner still has a sprinkler head, supply air and a light switch.
      • Window end. The wall meets a window mullion; we use a filler strip and seal, no drilling into the glass.
      • Ceiling. Measured at 8′4″ (2.54 m). The 8′ ready-made kit fits with 4″ to spare.
